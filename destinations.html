Destination Highlights & Seasons

The ideal season to visit Astana is from May to September when temperatures are warm and mild, perfect for exploring Nur Alem, Khan Shatyr, and Bayterek.
In winter, visit Shymbulak Ski Resort and Medeu. In summer, enjoy hiking near Big Almaty Lake, Kok-Tobe, and Charyn Canyon.
Best months are October through April. Don't miss Burj Khalifa, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, and desert safari experiences.
